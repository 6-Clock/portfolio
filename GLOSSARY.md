# Glossary

## Viewpoint

A named orientation of the hero pool paired with the **Placard** that describes
it — a Viewpoint carries both an azimuth and its Placard content. The pool has
three — **FrontLotus** (the resting front view), **Bird**, and **Dragonfly** —
arranged in a loop. A visitor steps between adjacent viewpoints, forward or
backward, and the pool rotates to face the chosen one while its Placard crossfades
in. Stepping past the last viewpoint wraps around to the first.

## Placard

A titled block of overlay text bound to a Viewpoint, shown lower-left and swapped
in when the visitor commits to that Viewpoint. Two kinds: **name** (a large
headline with no body, used for FrontLotus) and **prose** (an eyebrow title plus
a body paragraph, used for the About me / What I do views). The copy lives in the
single content config.
